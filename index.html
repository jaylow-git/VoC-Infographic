What we do next

Four moves that shift the number

1

Fix the VM Faults journey

Fix the VM Faults journey. Self-serve fell from a 42% winter average to 29% after the April API journey change. Restoring the diagnostics step is the single biggest recovery opportunity.

2

Target retentions demand

Target retentions demand. Cancellations and retentions is our biggest reason for contact at 788k conversations, and only 31.5% are Chatbot assisted. Journey redesign here moves the overall number.

3

Close the fallback gap

Close the fallback gap. Around 44k fallback contacts needed an agent in June. Expanding NLU coverage for unrecognised queries is a quick Chatbot assisted win.

4

Lift VM to O2's level

Lift VM to O2's level. VM trails O2 by 9 points on VA CSAT and on both trust measures. Applying what works on O2 journeys to VM is the clearest path to parity.
