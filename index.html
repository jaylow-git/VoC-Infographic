What we do next

Four moves that shift the number

1

Fix the VM Faults journey

Fix the VM Faults journey. Containment fell from a 42% winter average to 29% after the April API journey change. Restoring the diagnostics step is the single biggest recovery opportunity.

2

Target retentions demand

Target retentions demand. Cancel and retentions journeys leaked around 55,000 contacts to agents in June alone, more than any other intent. Journey redesign here moves the overall number.

3

Close the fallback gap

Close the fallback gap. Around 44,000 fallback contacts went uncontained in June. Expanding NLU coverage for unrecognised queries is a quick containment win.

4

Lift VM to O2's level

Lift VM to O2's level. VM trails O2 by 9 points on VA CSAT and on both trust measures. Applying what works on O2 journeys to VM is the clearest path to parity.
